## Abstract

While its democratic institutions remain resilient, recent elections (e.g., the 2026 Arizona Senate race) signal a potential shift toward Democratic control. Economically, the nation continues to innovate in AI, clean energy, and advanced manufacturing, yet faces inflation and supply-chain vulnerabilities. Socially, immigration, education reform, and public health will shape civic life. Globally, the U.S. is navigating a rivalry with China and a recalibrated security posture against Russia.
